OMEGA-ARC

Install guide — from zip to running in about 6 clicks (plus one model download)

Before you begin

ComputerWindows 10 or 11, 64-bit
Disk spaceAbout 20 GB free (the AI models are large)
Memory16 GB RAM recommended
InternetNeeded once, during setup, to download the AI models
Admin rightsNot needed — everything installs to your own user folder
1

Run the installer

Double-click OMEGA-ARC-Setup-0.3.2.exe from this folder.

Windows will warn you — this is expected. A blue box saying "Windows protected your PC" appears because this is independent software without a paid Microsoft certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway.
2

Click through the setup

Keep everything at its defaults and click Next through each page:

  • Leave the install location as suggested.
  • Leave "Download and install Ollama" checked — it is the engine that runs the AI models. (If you already have Ollama, this box won't appear.)
  • Keep "Create a desktop shortcut" checked so the icon lands on your desktop.

Setup takes a minute or two, including the Ollama download.

3

Launch it

On the final page, tick "Launch OMEGA-ARC now" and click Finish — or double-click the red-and-amber OMEGA-ARC icon on your desktop any time.

4

First launch: let it download the AI models

A console window opens and asks:

Download now? [Y/n]

Press Enter. It downloads two models (about 10 GB total) with a progress bar. This happens once — depending on your connection it can take 10–60 minutes. Leave the window open.

5

Done — your browser opens by itself

When everything is ready, your web browser opens the Command Deck at http://127.0.0.1:5173. Start typing — you are talking to your own private AI, running entirely on your machine. Nothing you say leaves your computer.

From now on, launching is instant: double-click the desktop icon and the browser opens when it's ready. The console window stays open in the background — closing it is fine (the runtime keeps running); to fully stop, restart your PC or end the processes in Task Manager.

If something goes wrong

"Ollama was not found" Install it from ollama.com/download, then double-click the OMEGA-ARC icon again.
Model download interrupted Just launch again — the download resumes where it stopped.
Responses are slow Normal on machines without a graphics card — the AI runs on your CPU. It thinks before it types.
Browser shows an error Wait ~30 seconds and refresh. Logs live in the logs folder inside the install directory.
Uninstalling Windows Settings → Apps → OMEGA-ARC → Uninstall. Your conversations and settings are kept; the downloaded models can be removed separately with ollama rm <model>.
